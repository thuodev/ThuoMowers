About Thuo Mowers

We started Thuo Mowers so busy families could get their weekends back. You live your life, and we take care of the garden.

Your weekends are for living, not mowing

In 2016, Thuo Kariuki was working long hours in Nairobi. Like most of his neighbours, he spent his only free Saturday morning pushing a mower around a compound that grew back faster than he could cut it.

One weekend a neighbour asked, half joking, whether he would mow hers too. She paid, told her friends, and by the end of the month Thuo had a waiting list. He realised he wasn't selling mowing. He was giving busy people their Saturday back.

That idea still runs the business. We show up when we said we would, bring our own equipment, and leave the compound tidier than we found it. You don't have to be home, supervise, or chase anyone for a quote.

Today our team looks after homes, estates, schools and offices across Kenya's major towns. The promise hasn't changed since that first neighbour: do it properly, do it on time, and give you your weekend back.
