Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about quotes, booking, rain and payment. Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

Quotes and pricing

How much does lawn mowing cost?

Prices start from KSh 1,000 for a small lawn. The final price depends on the size of your lawn, how overgrown it is and how easy it is to reach. Send us a message and we'll give you an exact price.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Quotes are free and come with no obligation, so you can compare before you decide.

Can you quote from a photo?

For most jobs, yes. Send a few photos and the town or estate on WhatsApp and we'll reply with a price. For large landscaping projects, we'll visit first so the quote is accurate.

Will the price change on the day?

No. The price on the day is the price we quoted. If the job turns out bigger than described, we'll tell you and agree the new price before doing any extra work.

Booking and scheduling

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp or use the quote form. Tell us your town or estate, what you need and roughly how big the garden is. We'll reply with a price and available days.

How soon can you come?

Often within a few days, depending on the season and your area. We'll give you an honest date when we send the quote.

Do I need to be home?

Not if we can reach the garden. Many customers leave a gate open or tell us where to enter, and pay afterwards by M-Pesa.

What happens if it rains?

Mowing wet grass gives a poor cut and can damage the lawn, so we reschedule to the next suitable day at no extra cost. We'll tell you as early as we can.

Can I set up regular visits?

Yes. Our maintenance plans cover weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits for one fixed monthly price. See the maintenance plans for details.

Payment

How do I pay?

After the job, by M-Pesa to our Till number or by cash. We'll send the Till details with your quote.

Do I have to pay a deposit?

Not for regular mowing or small jobs. For larger landscaping projects we may ask for a deposit toward materials, which we'll agree in the quote.

Do you give receipts?

Yes. Just ask and we'll send a receipt by WhatsApp or email.

The work

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes, everything. You don't need to provide a mower, tools or even a water connection.

Do you take away the cuttings and waste?

Yes. We clear cuttings, trimmings and rubbish from the work area before we leave.

Do you work in my area?

We serve all major towns and their estates. Check the full list on our areas page, or message us if you don't see yours.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we'll reply as soon as we can.
