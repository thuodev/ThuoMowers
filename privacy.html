Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have, in plain language.

In short

  • We never sell your details.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time.

1. Who we are

This website is run by [registered business name], trading as Thuo Mowers Services, based in [town], Kenya. We are the “data controller” for the personal data described below, which means we decide how and why it is used.

Contact for anything about your data: [name or role], email [privacy email address], phone [number].

2. What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us when you ask for a quote or contact us:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number (and email address, if you write to us)
  • Your town and estate, so we know where the job is
  • The service you want, plus the optional details you add: garden size, timing and any notes or photos
  • Your messages to us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email, and your payment details for the job (for example an M-Pesa confirmation message)

Please don't put anything sensitive in the notes box. We only need what is relevant to your garden.

3. How the quote form works

When you press the button on our quote form, your details are used to write a WhatsApp message to us. Our website does not store the form or send it to our servers. The message reaches us only when you press send in WhatsApp.

4. Why we use your details

  • To reply to your quote request and agree a price
  • To schedule, carry out and confirm the work
  • To take payment and keep records, including for tax
  • To deal with questions, complaints or follow-up about a job

We use your details because you asked us to (you ticked the consent box on the quote form or contacted us yourself), to do what you asked us to do, and where the law requires us to keep records. We do not use your details for marketing unless you separately tell us you want that.

5. Who else sees your details

  • Our team members who need them to do the job.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), because quote requests and chats travel through WhatsApp.
  • Safaricom (M-Pesa) if you pay by M-Pesa.
  • Google, which supplies the fonts used on this site. When a page loads, your browser contacts Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and device information.
  • Our website host, [host name], which keeps standard technical logs such as IP addresses.
  • Professional advisers or authorities, only where the law requires it.

We never sell your personal data.

6. Data sent outside Kenya

WhatsApp, Google and our website host may process data on servers outside Kenya. We use these services because they are widely used and have their own security and data protection arrangements, but their servers are not in our control. If you'd rather not use WhatsApp, you can phone or email us instead.

7. Cookies

This website does not currently set cookies to track you or measure visits. If we add analytics or similar tools in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

8. How long we keep your details

We keep quote requests that don't go ahead for [period, e.g. 6 months] and records of completed jobs and payments for [period, e.g. 7 years] to meet our tax and record-keeping duties. After that we delete them or remove anything that identifies you. [Confirm these periods with an accountant or lawyer.]

9. Keeping your details safe

We limit who can see your details, use passcodes on the phones and devices that hold our WhatsApp chats, and delete chats we no longer need. No system is perfectly secure, so if something goes wrong that puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Be told how your personal data is used
  • See the personal data we hold about you
  • Object to us using your data
  • Ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete data we no longer need
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We will reply within [period, e.g. 30 days] and will not charge you for a reasonable request.

11. If you are unhappy

Please speak to us first so we can put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya at www.odpc.go.ke.

12. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top. Please check it from time to time.
